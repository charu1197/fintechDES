FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before applying for a DES loan.

Does DES offer emergency loans?

DES offers four loan products — New Loan, Renewal Loan, Reloan, and Additional Loan — for housing, education, medical needs, and other important expenses. If your need is urgent, please contact our team and we will guide you to the right option.

Where are your branches located?

Our head office is at 181 Bonny Serrano Rd, Cubao, Brgy. Socorro, Quezon City 1109. You can see every branch on our branch locations page.

Is my loan insured?

Yes. DES loans are secure and insured. Please ask our team for the full details of your coverage.

Can I apply online or do I need to visit a branch?

Both are possible. You can inquire online, visit a branch, or talk to our team for guidance. If you apply online, courier your original documents via LBC to our Head Office.

What is the maximum age to apply for a loan?

Age requirements depend on the loan product. Please contact our team to confirm the requirements that apply to you.

What documents do I need to apply?

Principal pensioners: AFP Pensioner's ID, one valid government ID, Retirement Order, two 2×2 colored photos, and Landbank ATM card. Widows and widowers also need the Posthumous/Retirement Order, Declaration of Beneficiary, PSA Marriage Certificate, PSA Death Certificate, and CENOMAR (Husband and Wife).
